## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-31

First public release for the original Canon EOS M, Canon firmware 2.0.2, with the Magic Lantern 2025-06-20 EOSM.202 build.

- Intentional Bayer-plane misregistration saved directly in Canon CR2 RAW photographs.
- Subtle, Crunchy, Overdrive and Custom recipes; Strength, Pattern, Texture size, Phase and Shuffle controls.
- Persistent settings and recipe freezing on the physical shutter press.
- Packed 14-bit RAW processing with a 39,600-byte workspace.
- Normal Canon LiveView for composing and focusing; no effect preview or per-frame processing.
- Guarded native capture, RAW readback verification and cancellation handling.

The release promotes the capture-only development implementation. Camera operation on the EOS M was confirmed by Max Seelig. Result photographs are pending and are not included as validation evidence.
