## Design Context

### Users
Creative photographers with an original Canon EOS M. They want to see real Bayer Drift photographs and their tiny colour patterns, check the supported setup, download the module and start shooting.

### Brand Personality
Playful, tactile, curious. By Max Seelig (MAXmade). The creator requested a landing page like Mirror Worlds and supplied original CR2 photographs specifically for a showcase with enlarged details.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the established Mirror Worlds photography-zine identity: warm paper, dark ink, assertive Bricolage display type and Public Sans text. Inherit its existing font assets and licences rather than selecting a new type system. Give Bayer Drift its own saturated tomato accent and Bayer-cell mark. Let real colour interference do the visual work. Photographic evidence and a useful pixel viewer come before technical explanation.

### Design Principles
- Lead with genuine RAW-developed photographs, with accurately matched detail crops.
- Make magnification useful on touch screens and with a keyboard.
- Keep one clear download path and calm installation instructions.
- State normal Canon LiveView and original EOS M support plainly.
- Do not invent before/after pairs, recipe labels or camera settings that are absent from the supplied files.
- Serve light web derivatives; keep original CR2 files off the public site.
